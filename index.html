Embed the removals timeline

A timeline of every arrangement to transfer people to a country they are not from, concluded since January 2025. It is one self-contained file — put it in an iframe and it works. Copy a snippet below.

The code

Paste this wherever raw HTML is allowed — a custom HTML block, a template, a page source. Nothing else to install.

Standard

<iframe src="https://osinv.org/embed/graph.html"
        title="A Timeline of Removals and Related Deals"
        width="100%" height="760"
        style="border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Responsive height

<div style="position:relative;width:100%;aspect-ratio:4/3;min-height:420px">
  <iframe src="https://osinv.org/embed/graph.html"
          title="A Timeline of Removals and Related Deals"
          style="position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%;border:0"
          loading="lazy"></iframe>
</div>

Keep the title attribute. It is the only name a screen reader has for the frame, and an untitled iframe is a plain accessibility failure on your page, not ours.

Sizing

The graph fills whatever box you give it and never scrolls your page. It has no fixed aspect ratio — it re-lays out at every size, dropping the dates under each stop before it lets any region fall off the bottom.

Recommended
Full column width, 760 px tall.
Comfortable
640 px wide and up, 560–900 px tall.
Minimum
320 px wide, 300 px tall. It holds together, but readers will lean on the zoom.
Narrower than 700 px
Phone layout: the standfirst and the legend give way to the map, the filters collapse behind a Filters button, region names go abbreviated, marks get finger-sized targets and the view opens on the most recent five months. Reset still shows the full record.

How readers use it

  • Click any mark for the agreement, its terms and its sources.
  • Drag sideways to pan. Hold ⌘ or Ctrl while scrolling to zoom — a bare scroll is left alone, so nobody gets trapped in the frame on the way down your page. A trackpad pinch works without the modifier.
  • The − / + / Reset buttons do the same thing, and the filter chips switch whole categories on and off.
  • Keyboard: tab to the chart, then + − to zoom, arrow keys to pan, 0 for the full record. Tab reaches every mark; Enter opens it; Escape closes.

What it loads

Requests
The page itself, plus Google Fonts. Everything else — all data, the logo, every script — is inside the one file.
If fonts are blocked
It falls back to Georgia, Helvetica and your system monospace, and still lays out correctly.
Cookies and tracking
None. No analytics, no third-party scripts, no storage.
Accessibility
Keyboard operable throughout, WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, every mark carries a screen-reader name, and each category is a distinct shape rather than only a colour.
Page weight
275 KB uncompressed; 74 KB over the wire with gzip, 60 KB with brotli. Most of it is the data itself.

Hosting it yourself

If you would rather not hot-link, download graph.html and drop it on any static host. It has no build step and no server requirement — file:// works too. Point your iframe at your own copy. A self-hosted copy will not pick up corrections, so prefer the canonical URL if you can.
